01 / Cross-cutting capability

Strategy

Information, criteria and alternatives for growth, investment and transformation decisions. Applied to companies, regions, economic sectors and development programs.

Services 4

01Market and demand studies

Analysis of sectors, competitors, customers and users, with estimates of size, trends and commercial potential. Quantitative and qualitative research to identify opportunities and support projects, investments and policies.

02Assessments and feasibility studies

Comprehensive evaluation of situations and development alternatives. Technical, operational, commercial, institutional and economic viability, identifying constraints, risks and requirements to move forward.

03Business models and investment strategies

Definition of value propositions, revenue models, cost structures and ways of operating. Comparison of scenarios to guide new activities, expansion, diversification and capital decisions.

04Strategic and development plans

Objectives, priorities, initiatives and implementation sequences for organizations, production chains and regions. Growth, transformation and investment programs with resources, responsibilities and monitoring criteria.

02 / Cross-cutting capability

Culture, Leadership and Organization

Leadership, coordination and operational capabilities to support the performance and evolution of organizations. Integration of organizational, human and technical approaches.

Ask about this specialty →

Services 4

01Organizational assessment and design

Analysis of structures, responsibilities, working relationships and decision-making. Configuration of management models, coordination mechanisms and indicators suited to the strategy and scale of each institution.

02Process engineering and improvement

Evaluation of operational and administrative workflows to increase productivity, quality and reliability. Redesign of tasks, interfaces, resources and information systems, with implementation and monitoring criteria.

03Education and training programs

Development of technical, leadership and management skills based on specific needs. Learning paths, content, learning formats and mechanisms to verify their application on the job.

04Leadership development and organizational transformation

Strengthening of management teams, leadership practices and collaboration across areas. Support for cultural and management change, with attention to the adoption of new responsibilities and ways of working.

03 / Cross-cutting capability

Project Design and Management

Technical and management capabilities throughout the investment cycle, from the definition of an initiative to its contracting, implementation and control.

Ask about this specialty →

Services 5

01Project formulation and appraisal

Definition of objectives, needs, scope and alternative solutions. Preliminary studies, sizing, cost estimates, risk analysis and phase planning to prepare investment decisions.

02Architecture, urban planning and master plans

Proposals for spatial organization, uses, site layout and development phases. Preliminary designs and design documentation for buildings, complexes, large sites and urban areas, coordinating the disciplines involved.

03Technical studies and engineering

Development of design criteria, functional analyses and conceptual, basic or detailed solutions depending on the stage of development. Specifications, budgets and interdisciplinary coordination for their evaluation and execution.

04Technical documentation and procurement processes

Preparation of terms of reference, tender documents, specifications and budgets. Organization of tenders and competitions, support with bidder queries and evaluation of offers for purchases, works and professional services.

05Project management and control

Direction and monitoring of scope, time, cost, quality and risk. Coordination of teams and contractors, interface management and progress documentation to support decisions during execution.

04 / Cross-cutting capability

Legal and Financial Structuring

Economic, corporate and contractual instruments to organize investments, allocate responsibilities and make projects with multiple participants viable.

Ask about this specialty →

Services 4

01Economic and financial modeling and evaluation

Projections of revenues, expenditures, investment and financing; cash flows, return indicators and sensitivity analysis. Comparison of alternatives and risks to support decisions and negotiations.

02Legal structuring of projects and investments

Design of vehicles, participation schemes and the allocation of rights, obligations and contingencies. Consideration of the regulatory frameworks and institutional conditions of each initiative.

03Contracts and negotiation processes

Preparation and review of instruments for investments, services, developments and construction works. Commercial terms, commitments, guarantees and mechanisms for resolving disputes between the parties.

04Financial structuring and participation models

Equity, debt and hybrid alternatives, including public-private partnership arrangements. Organization of contributions, returns and agreements among owners, investors, operators and funding institutions.
