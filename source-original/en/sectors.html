Sectors

Sector knowledge and a systems view.

Our practice is organized around sectors where experience, networks and delivery capability come together. In each one, our services run from early-stage studies through to project management.

Industrial and CorporateUrban and Real Estate DevelopmentInfrastructure and Transport

01 / Sector

Industrial and Corporate

Services for industrial, commercial and service companies in their growth, performance improvement and investment processes. Capabilities in commercial strategy, economic management, organization, engineering and project delivery, integrated according to the needs of the business.

Ask about this sector →

Services 4

01Process and facility assessment and engineering

Assessment of production, logistics and corporate operations and assets to improve capacity, productivity and reliability. Redesign of workflows, modernization, expansions and relocations, with feasibility studies, architecture and engineering solutions and investment estimates.

02Organizational design, leadership and training

Structures, responsibilities and management models suited to each company’s needs. Development of management teams, coordination across areas, expense control and training programs linked to growth and changes in operations.

03Commercial expansion and expense and cost optimization

Growth strategies, market development, sales channels and new business opportunities. Analysis of profitability, expense structure, operating costs, purchasing and procurement, with economic and commercial improvement plans that strengthen competitiveness and guide management and investment decisions.

04Management of transformation and investment projects

Planning and leadership of modernization, expansion and business change initiatives. Budgets, schedules, contracting, team coordination and monitoring of execution, with attention to meeting objectives, operational continuity and expected results.

02 / Sector

Urban and Real Estate Development

Services for private, public and mixed developments involving land, buildings and development areas that require the integration of urban planning, technical and economic criteria. Studies, planning, projects and investment structures to advance viable initiatives and coordinate their participants.

Ask about this sector →

Services 4

01Urban and real estate feasibility studies

Assessment of assets and opportunities based on their territorial context, regulatory framework, demand and economic conditions. Alternative uses and development potential, commercial scenarios and investment estimates to support development decisions.

02Master plans, architecture and engineering

Definition of uses, site layouts, phasing and infrastructure for sites, urban complexes and real estate developments. Development of spatial proposals and technical solutions, coordinating disciplines with levels of design suited to each project.

03Legal and financial structuring of developments

Participation and investment models to bring together landowners, developers, operators and funders. Economic evaluation, organization of contributions and returns, corporate instruments and contractual agreements that make each initiative viable.

04Real estate project management

Organization and coordination of developments from definition through execution. Management of technical teams, budgets, timelines, procurement and contracting, tracking milestones, risks and commitments between the parties.

03 / Sector

Infrastructure and Transport

Studies and projects to improve connectivity, the operation of networks and the performance of logistics and transport systems. Planning, engineering, investment structuring and program management in public, private and inter-institutional settings.

Ask about this sector →

Services 4

01Demand and mobility studies

Characterization of freight and passenger flows, operating patterns, connectivity needs and the evolution of markets and regions. Projections and scenarios to size services, identify constraints and guide investment.

02Logistics planning and infrastructure engineering

Assessment of corridors, hubs, networks and operating systems, focusing on capacity, costs, times and reliability. Organizational and location alternatives, functional solutions, preliminary designs and technical documentation for their development.

03Structuring of infrastructure investments and contracts

Analysis of economic viability, financing schemes, regulatory frameworks and allocation of responsibilities. Public-private participation models, tender documents and agreements to prepare projects and facilitate their execution.

04Project and program management

Technical, institutional and contractual coordination of investments of different scales. Organization of teams, schedules, budgets and procurement processes; physical and financial monitoring, interface management and progress control.
